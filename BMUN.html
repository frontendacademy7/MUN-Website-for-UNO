About UNO

One classroom, every country at the table.

Model United Nations (MUN) is an educational simulation of the United Nations where students represent different countries and discuss global issues. It gives students the opportunity to experience international diplomacy, debate important world problems, and find peaceful solutions through discussion. In our MUN, every participant takes on the role of a delegate representing a country. Delegates research their assigned country's policies, interests, and position on global issues. They then participate in formal debates, negotiations, and collaborative discussions. MUN helps students develop confidence, public speaking, leadership, and critical thinking skills. It also teaches the importance of teamwork, respect, diplomacy, and understanding different perspectives. Through this experience, students learn how the United Nations works and how countries cooperate. Most importantly, MUN encourages young people to think globally and become responsible future leaders.

The section exists to give students a structured, competitive space to practice diplomacy: drafting resolutions, forming blocs, defending a country's stance under real time pressure, and reaching consensus with people who disagree with them.

It also runs like a real organisation. Every committee needs a chair to keep order, a secretariat to keep it running, and delegates who've done the homework — which is exactly the structure below.
